Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Who We Are

Slide & Solve is an independent mobile puzzle game built and maintained by our developer team (“we”, “us”, or “our”). We are committed to protecting player privacy and ensuring a safe, transparent, and enjoyable puzzle-solving experience. If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach us directly at kalpvarti@gmail.com or by phone at +91 9537261607.

2. Information Handled by the App

Slide & Solve is built with privacy by design. The game is an offline-capable number puzzle that does not require you to create an account, log in, or submit personal details to play.

  • Personal data: We do not collect, request, or store your name, email address, physical address, or phone number during gameplay.
  • Device permissions: Slide & Solve uses only minimal non-sensitive permissions, such as vibration for haptic feedback when sliding puzzle tiles. We do not access sensitive hardware or data such as your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or GPS location.
  • Gameplay data: Your game statistics (including timer records, move counts, chosen puzzle dimensions from 3x3 to 8x8, and sound/haptic toggles) are stored locally on your own device. This data is never sent to or sold on private external servers.
  • App store telemetry: If you download the app through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, those platforms may handle standard installation metrics and anonymized crash reporting in accordance with their standard developer policies.

3. How Information Is Used

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not use your information for advertising profiles, data monetization, or tracking across other apps and websites. Any local gameplay data on your device is used strictly to remember your preferences and display your best scores across puzzle sessions.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Core gameplay operates independently on your device without transmitting private personal records.

5. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

All puzzle records, high scores, and user preferences are retained locally in your device’s app storage. You can delete all gameplay data at any time by clearing the application storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You have full control over your experience:

  • You can customize or reset your game statistics and puzzle board settings inside the game at any time.
  • You can toggle sound effects and haptic vibration feedback on or off in the game settings.
  • For any privacy-related requests or questions, you can contact us at any time at kalpvarti@gmail.com.

7. Children’s Privacy

Slide & Solve is a family-friendly educational number puzzle suitable for players of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect, request, or store personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable age threshold in your region). If you believe that a child has shared personal information with us, please reach out so we can promptly address your concern.

8. Security

Because your game progress is stored locally on your device, it is secured by your device’s operating system security features. When you reach out to us via email or phone, we treat your communications with confidentiality and care.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect future features or changes in legal regulations. When updates are published, the revised version will always be posted at https://slide-solve.kalpvarti.com/privacy-policy with the updated effective date.
